## Symptoms

A greenish-brown to black circular patch at the flower end of the fruit, which becomes sunken and
flattened as it grows.

## Cause and management

Soils rarely lack calcium; the fruit misses out when the water flow through the plant is uneven.
Plants in pots and growing bags are most at risk. Water regularly and consistently so the root zone
never dries out; later fruit usually develops normally.
