## Symptoms

Fruit rots with a powdery grey fungal growth, often starting at the stem end; rotting spreads to
berries that touch and the fruit dries into shrivelled "mummies".

## Management

- Plant in full sun with good air movement and do not crowd plants.
- Water at ground level, early in the day; straw under the plants keeps fruit off wet soil.
- Pick often and remove rotten fruit; keep it apart from healthy fruit.
- In beds with a history of grey mould, replace old straw in early spring.
