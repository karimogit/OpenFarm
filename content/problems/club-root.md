## Symptoms

Stunted plants with purplish leaves that wilt in hot weather; roots swollen, distorted and missing
their fine roots.

## Management

- Buy brassica plants only from a club-root-free source.
- On infected ground, raise plants to a larger size in pots (at least 9 cm) before planting out.
- Do not move infected soil around on tools, wheelbarrows or boots.
