## Symptoms

- Leaves: olive-green, velvety spots that darken; leaves may drop early.
- Twigs: blistering and cracking, an entry point for canker.
- Fruit: brown to black scabby blotches, sometimes causing cracks and poor storage.

## Management

Choose scab-resistant varieties, and clear up fallen leaves in autumn to reduce next year's spores.
Light attacks only mark the skin.
