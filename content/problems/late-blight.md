## Symptoms

- A fast-spreading, watery rot of the leaves, which collapse, shrivel and turn brown; in humid
  weather a fine white growth shows under the leaves.
- Brown lesions on stems and leaf stalks.
- Tomatoes: brown patches on green fruit and rapid decay of ripening fruit.
- Potatoes: reddish-brown rot under the skin of tubers, often followed by soft rot in store.

## Management

- Grow resistant varieties where available, give plants space and air, and water at the base.
- Grow tomatoes under cover where blight is common – greenhouse plants are less often affected.
- Remove infected potato foliage promptly and do not leave infected tubers in the ground, where the
  pathogen overwinters.
- Keep tomatoes and potatoes apart: it is the same disease on both crops.
