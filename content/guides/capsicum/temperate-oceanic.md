## Summary

Sweet peppers need warmth from sowing until the fruit colours. Outdoors they only belong in a sheltered spot after frost; a greenhouse is the reliable crop in a cool summer.

## Sowing and planting

Sow under cover in February–April. Plant in April–June. In the row, allow 38–45 cm. Do not put frost-tender plants outside until the frost risk has passed.

## Harvest

Harvest in July–October.

An April planting is for a warm greenhouse. Wait until frost has passed before they go outside. Space plants 38–45 cm apart, or about 30 cm for a compact variety.
