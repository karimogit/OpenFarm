## Summary

Blueberries need acid soil. In alkaline ground, grow them in a large pot of ericaceous compost rather than the open bed.

## Sowing and planting

Plant in October–March. In the row, allow 100–200 cm.

## Harvest

Harvest in July–September.

They fruit best in sun and still give a reasonable crop in partial shade, if the compost stays moist. Space bushes about 1–2 m apart, depending on the variety. Net the fruit as it colours, or birds will take it. Plant while dormant.
