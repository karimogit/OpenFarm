## Timing

Colorado State University groups this crop as **very tender**: plant 2 or more weeks after the average last frost (15 May), once days stay above about 13 °C (55 °F). Typical days to harvest: 60 from transplanting.

The average frost dates on the Front Range are 15 May (last) and 15 October (first) according to CSU PlantTalk; harvest months are estimated and stop at the autumn frost.
