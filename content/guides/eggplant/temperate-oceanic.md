## Summary

Aubergines need a long, warm season. In a cool oceanic summer they belong in a greenhouse, a polytunnel or the warmest spot against a wall, not in an open bed.

## Sowing and planting

Sow under cover in January–March. Plant in May–June. In the row, allow 60 cm. Do not put frost-tender plants outside until the frost risk has passed.

## Harvest

Harvest in August–October.

Pinch the tip once the plant is about 30 cm tall so it branches. Space plants about 60 cm apart.
