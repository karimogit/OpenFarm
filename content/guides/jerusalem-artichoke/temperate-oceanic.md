## Summary

Jerusalem artichokes are grown from tubers and make a tall summer screen. Dig the crop once the tops have died.

## Sowing and planting

Plant in February–May. In the row, allow 30 cm.

## Harvest

Harvest in November–February.

Plant tubers 10–15 cm deep and about 30 cm apart, from late winter into spring. Lift them thoroughly: leftover pieces regrow, and in light soil the tubers spread wide and go deep. They can stay in the ground through winter where the soil is not frozen hard.
