## Summary

Globe artichokes are large perennials. In a mild winter they stay in the ground and crop for several years.

## Sowing and planting

Sow under cover in February–May. Plant in May–June. In the row, allow 75–90 cm.

## Harvest

Harvest in June–September.

Start seed under cover and plant out once the young plants are about 30 cm tall with several leaves, or buy young plants. Leave 75–90 cm between plants. Cut the bud while it is still tight.
