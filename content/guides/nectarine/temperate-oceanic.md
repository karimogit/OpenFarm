## Summary

Nectarines flower early and fruit on a warm wall, not in an open, windy garden. A cool wet spring brings peach leaf curl, so a cover over the fan from winter into spring is part of growing them here.

## Sowing and planting

Plant in November–March.

## Harvest

Harvest in July–September.

Plant dormant trees 20–30 cm out from the wall. Thin fruitlets to about 15 cm apart. Harvest is mid to late summer in a good year.
