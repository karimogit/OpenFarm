## Timing

Colorado State University groups this crop as **tender**: plant around the average last frost (15 May); transplants of cucumber and summer squash only once frost risk is over. Typical days to harvest: 50.

The average frost dates on the Front Range are 15 May (last) and 15 October (first) according to CSU PlantTalk; harvest months are estimated and stop at the autumn frost.
