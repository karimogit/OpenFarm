## Summary

This guide follows the Pima County row for summer squash, which includes
zucchini. Sow or transplant is marked from April through mid-August. Harvest is
marked from September through early November. The chart does not mark a midsummer
harvest for this row.

## Timing

- Sow or transplant: April through mid-August.
- Harvest: September, October and early November.
