## Summary

Courgettes are heavy feeders and need room. One or two plants feed a household if you keep cutting.

## Sowing and planting

Sow under cover in April–May. Sow outdoors in May–June. Plant in May–June. In the row, allow 90 cm. Between rows, allow 90 cm. Do not put frost-tender plants outside until the frost risk has passed.

## Harvest

Harvest in July–October.

Leave at least 90 cm each way. Sow pots under cover, or put seed straight into warm soil once frost is gone. Cut fruit small and often; a fruit left to swell slows the plant. A mature fruit is a marrow, and the same plants cover that use.
