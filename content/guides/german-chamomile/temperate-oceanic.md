## Summary

German chamomile is the annual chamomile, sown in spring for a summer flush of flowers. The RHS page also describes the perennial lawn chamomile, which is a different plant.

## Sowing and planting

Sow outdoors in March–May. Plant in May–June. In the row, allow 15 cm.

## Harvest

Harvest in July–September.

Thin seedlings to about 15 cm. Pick flower heads in summer, while they are open, if you want them for tea.
