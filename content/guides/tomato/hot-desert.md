## Summary

The low desert has two short tomato seasons, spring and autumn, separated by a summer that is too
hot for fruit set. Tomatoes thrive between about 10 °C and 32 °C (50–90 °F); above 32 °C pollen
viability drops and flowers fail to set fruit.

## Timing

- **Spring crop**: transplant **mid-February to mid-March**. The main growing period runs from
  mid-March to late June.
- **Autumn crop**: transplant from **mid-July to 1 August** (shade young plants), or start seed in
  June. The autumn season runs from September until the first frost in late November.
- Alternatively, cut back straggly spring plants in August for a second crop, or root cuttings of
  spring plants in water about three weeks before the autumn planting.

## Varieties

Choose early-maturing types (about **45–70 days**) and look for "heat tolerant", "early", cherry,
grape or plum types.

## Care

Expect poor fruit development in July–August. Water deeply and regularly; irrigation is essential.

## Harvest

Spring plantings ripen roughly April to June; autumn plantings from October until frost.
