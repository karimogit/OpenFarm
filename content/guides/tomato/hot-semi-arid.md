## Summary

The Pima County chart gives tomato two seasons, with a gap through the hottest
part of the summer. Sow seeds are marked in January to early March and again in
mid-June to early July. Transplants are marked from mid-February through April
and from mid-July through mid-August.

The chart does not say whether winter “Sow Seeds” cells are outdoor sowings or
starts under cover. Harvest is marked only in mid-May and in October to early
November.

## Timing

- Sow seeds: January, February, early March; mid-June and early July.
- Transplant: mid-February through April; mid-July through mid-August.
- Harvest (green Harvest cells): mid-May; October and early November.
