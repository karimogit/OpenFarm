## Summary

Mild, wet winters and long, hot, dry summers. Tomatoes are a summer crop that
depends on irrigation, because there is little or no rain from late spring to autumn.

## Sowing and planting

- Start seeds indoors in **February–March**.
- Plant transplants out when the soil is around **18 °C (65 °F)** – in the Sacramento
  Valley usually **late April to early May**. UC lists April–May for interior valleys.

## Care

- Water deeply and regularly through the dry summer; drip irrigation and mulch
  save water and keep leaves dry.
- Some afternoon shade (shade cloth) can protect fruit from sunscald in heat waves.

## Harvest

**July to October**, sometimes into November in mild autumns.

## Problems

- Heat stress and blossom drop during extreme heat.
- Spider mites in dry heat; tomato hornworm in some regions.
