## Summary

Carrot can be grown year-round in Hawaiʻi, but quality is better in the cooler months.
CTAHR gives an optimum of about 16–21 °C (60–70 °F). At warmer lowland temperatures,
roots tend to be shorter, lighter in colour and stronger in flavour than the same
variety grown higher up.

## Timing

Direct-sow year-round. CTAHR harvests about four months after planting; roots left
longer become woody and tough. Getting a stand is the main difficulty: seed is small
and germinates slowly, and seedlings fail if the soil crusts.

## Spacing and water

Sow ¼ inch deep in heavy soils or ½ inch in light soils, in rows 12–15 inches
(30–38 cm) apart, and thin to 2–4 inches (5–10 cm) between plants. Keep a steady
water supply; long drought followed by irrigation can crack roots, and overwatering
can roughen them.
