## Summary

UNR Extension includes carrots with the cool-season crops that can be
direct-seeded in the Reno area until the end of May, after the mid-March opening
for peas and spinach. Carrots are also named as a crop that can be sown more
than once to stretch the harvest.

## Sowing

- Direct-seed from April through the end of May.
- The fact sheet’s succession example is a later sowing two to four weeks after
  the first, which stays inside that spring window.
- A site with full sun for at least six to eight hours and a steady water supply
  is advised; drip irrigation is commonly used in the area.

## Harvest

No harvest table is published. June–July is listed from the April–May sowing
window only.
