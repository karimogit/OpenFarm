## Summary

Carrots are direct-sown on the Pima County chart from January through April and
again from September through December. The only harvest cells marked are in May.
Autumn sowings are therefore listed here against that same May harvest window in
the following year; the chart does not mark a separate autumn harvest.

## Timing

- Sow seeds: January–April; September–December.
- Harvest: May only, as marked on the January 2025 chart.
