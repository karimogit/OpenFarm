## Summary

Mint is hardy and spreads by runners. Plant it where that spread is welcome, or keep it in a sunken pot.

## Sowing and planting

Plant in March–May, September–November.

## Harvest

Harvest in May–October.

Plant in spring or autumn. It grows in sun or partial shade if the soil stays moist. Cut the clump back to about 5 cm when it gets tired and it will shoot again. The calendar does not list a seed sowing; plants and divisions are the usual start.
