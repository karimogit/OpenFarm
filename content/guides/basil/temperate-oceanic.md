## Summary

Basil is a warmth crop. Outdoors it only settles once summer is properly underway; a pot on a sunny sill is the reliable plant.

## Sowing and planting

Sow under cover in March–July. Plant in June–July. In the row, allow 10–20 cm. Do not put frost-tender plants outside until the frost risk has passed.

## Harvest

Harvest in June–October.

Sow under cover and do not plant outside before June. Space plants 10–20 cm apart. Pinch tips often so the plant stays leafy, and bring pots inside before the first frost.
