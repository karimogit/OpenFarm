## Summary

Rhubarb is a perennial crown. Plant it while it is dormant and then leave it alone long enough to establish.

## Sowing and planting

Plant in November–March. In the row, allow 75–90 cm.

## Harvest

Harvest in March–July.

Space crowns 75–90 cm apart. Take no stalks in the first year, only a few in the second, and from the third year up to about a third of the stalks at one time. Pull stalks rather than cutting into the crown. The leaf blade is not the crop.
