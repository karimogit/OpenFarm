## Summary

This guide follows the chart row for bulb onions, not the separate bunching or
scallion rows. Transplants are marked in January and again in late November and
December; sow seeds are marked in October–November. Harvest is marked only in
June.

## Timing

- Sow seeds: October and November.
- Transplant: January; late November and December.
- Harvest: June only, as marked on the bulb-onion row.
