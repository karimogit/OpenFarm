## Summary

Bulb onions grow well in a warm, sunny, well-drained oceanic garden, from sets
or from seed. The Royal Horticultural Society treats sets as the easiest method.
A limited range of overwintering or Japanese sets is sold in autumn for an
earlier crop.

## Sowing and planting

- Sets: plant in spring, 10–15 cm apart in rows 20–30 cm apart, with the tip
  just showing. Autumn (overwintering) sets give a June–July harvest. If the
  soil is very wet and cold, start sets in modules and move them out later.
- Seed indoors: sow mid-winter to early spring, about 1.3 cm deep, and plant out
  in spring at the same spacing. Some overwintering varieties can be sown
  indoors in late summer or early autumn.
- Seed outdoors: sow early to mid-spring, then thin to 10 cm.

## Harvest

Autumn-planted sets are ready in June and July; spring-planted sets and
seed-raised onions from July to October. Lift when the foliage yellows and
starts to topple, before it dies down completely, and dry the bulbs before
storage.
