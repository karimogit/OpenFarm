## Summary

Radicchio is the red heading chicory. On the chicory calendar it is sown with the other non-forcing types and cut as baby leaves or as a firm head.

## Sowing and planting

Sow outdoors in February–October. Plant in May–June. In the row, allow 30 cm. Between rows, allow 30 cm.

## Harvest

Harvest in July–March.

Treat it as a non-forcing chicory: about 30 cm between plants and rows. Heads colour up towards the end of summer. A bitter leaf is normal; the inner heart is milder.
