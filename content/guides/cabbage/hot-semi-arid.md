## Summary

This guide follows the chart row for traditional heading cabbage (not the
separate Chinese/Napa or Savoy rows, which have slightly different autumn sowing
starts). Sow or transplant is marked in January–February and from August through
December. Harvest is marked in March and April. Autumn sowings line up with that
spring harvest window in the following year; the chart does not mark a separate
autumn harvest for this row.

## Timing

- Sow or transplant: January–February; August–December (transplant is marked
  with seed from November).
- Harvest: March and April.
