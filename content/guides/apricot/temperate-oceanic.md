## Summary

Apricots flower very early. In this climate they need a warm, sheltered wall, or frost on the blossom takes the crop.

## Sowing and planting

Plant in November–March.

## Harvest

Harvest in July–August.

Plant while the tree is dormant, with the roots about 30 cm out from a wall if you are training it. Fruit, when you get it, ripens in mid to late summer. A fleece over blossom on a frosty night is worth more than extra feed.
