## Summary

Celery wants rich, moist soil and a season without a check in growth. Self-blanching types are the usual choice for a home garden.

## Sowing and planting

Sow under cover in March–April. Plant in May–June. In the row, allow 23–45 cm.

## Harvest

Harvest in July–December.

A block at about 23 cm each way lets the plants shade one another. In a single row, leave 30–45 cm. Keep the soil watered; a dry spell makes the stalks stringy.
