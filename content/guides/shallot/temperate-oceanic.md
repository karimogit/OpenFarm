## Summary

Shallots are usually grown from sets. Seed is on the calendar too, but sets are the simpler start.

## Sowing and planting

Sow outdoors in February, April–May. Plant in October–April. In the row, allow 15–20 cm. Between rows, allow 25–30 cm.

## Harvest

Harvest in July–August.

Plant sets in autumn or from late winter into spring, 15–20 cm apart with 25–30 cm between rows, tips just at the surface. Lift bulbs once the tops fall, and dry them before storing.
