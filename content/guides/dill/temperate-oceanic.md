## Summary

Dill is an annual for leaves and seed. It dislikes being moved, so sow it where it will grow.

## Sowing and planting

Sow outdoors in March–July. Plant in April–June. In the row, allow 20 cm. Between rows, allow 30 cm.

## Harvest

Harvest in May–October.

Sow about 1 cm deep, thin to about 20 cm, and leave 30 cm between rows. Leaf is ready first; seed follows the flowers.
