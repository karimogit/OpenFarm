## Summary

Medlars are an easy, small fruit tree. The fruit is picked hard and is not eaten until it has softened.

## Sowing and planting

Plant in November–March.

## Harvest

Harvest in October–November.

Plant while dormant. Harvest in October and November, while the fruit is still firm, then store it for several weeks until it is dark, soft and aromatic. That softening is the bletting the fruit needs.
