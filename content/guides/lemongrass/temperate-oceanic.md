## Summary

Lemongrass is a tender grass. In this climate it is a summer plant outdoors and needs to come inside, or be treated as an annual, before winter.

## Sowing and planting

Sow under cover in March–April. Plant in June. In the row, allow 30 cm. Do not put frost-tender plants outside until the frost risk has passed.

## Harvest

Harvest in May–September.

Sow under cover in early spring and plant out in June, at least 30 cm apart, once nights are warm. Cut stalks in summer. Before frost, pot a piece up or expect the clump to die outside.
