## Summary

Bay is an evergreen shrub. In a cold snap a pot can be moved; a plant in the ground wants a sheltered wall in the colder parts of this climate.

## Sowing and planting

Plant in March–May.

## Harvest

The harvest row is marked in every month.

Plant in spring. Leaves can be picked in any month once the shrub is established. A hard winter can cut growth back; established plants in a mild site usually recover.
