## Summary

Sweet cherries crop in this climate if blossom misses a hard frost and the fruit is netted against birds. Many modern trees are self-fertile, so one tree can be enough.

## Sowing and planting

Plant in November–March.

## Harvest

Harvest in June–August.

Plant while dormant. A fan on a wall suits a small garden; leave 25–35 cm between the stem and the wall. Fruit ripens in early to mid summer. The same RHS page covers acid cherries, which are listed separately as sour cherry.
