## Summary

Turnips give a quick root and, if you sow them for the tops, a leaf crop as well. Small roots are milder than large ones.

## Sowing and planting

Sow outdoors in February–August. Plant in March–April. In the row, allow 10–15 cm. Between rows, allow 23–30 cm.

## Harvest

Harvest in May–December.

Sow about 1 cm deep. Thin maincrop roots to about 15 cm, with 23–30 cm between rows. Leaves can be sown more thickly. Roots and tops both sit on the harvest calendar into winter.
