## Summary

Chard is a leaf beet that stands longer than spinach and gives both leaf and stem. It is one of the more forgiving greens in a wet summer.

## Sowing and planting

Sow outdoors in March–August. Plant in May. In the row, allow 30 cm. Between rows, allow 45 cm.

## Harvest

Harvest in March–November.

Sow about 2.5 cm deep from spring into summer. Thin to about 30 cm, with 45 cm between rows, or much closer if you only want baby leaves. Pick outer leaves and leave the crown to grow on.
