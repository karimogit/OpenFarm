## Summary

Sweetcorn is wind-pollinated, so plant a block rather than one long row. It needs warm soil and a frost-free summer.

## Sowing and planting

Sow under cover in April–May. Sow outdoors in May–June. Plant in May–June. In the row, allow 34–45 cm. Between rows, allow 34–45 cm. Do not put frost-tender plants outside until the frost risk has passed.

## Harvest

Harvest in August–October.

Sow 2.5 cm deep, in pots under cover or outdoors in a grid at 34–45 cm each way once the soil has warmed. Baby-corn varieties can be closer, about 20 cm, in rows.
