## Summary

Lemon balm is a hardy perennial mint relative. It spreads by seed if you let it flower, so cut it back if you want it to stay put.

## Sowing and planting

Sow outdoors in March–June. Plant in March–May.

## Harvest

Harvest in April–October.

Sow or plant in spring. Pick leaves through the growing season. A pot keeps a small clump from taking the bed.
