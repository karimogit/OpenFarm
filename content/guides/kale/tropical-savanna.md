## Summary

A cool-season green that only fits the cooler, drier months in the lowland tropics.

## Planting

UF/IFAS (South Florida): plant **September–January**, 8–12 inches (20–30 cm) apart in
rows 18 inches (45 cm) apart; 50–70 days to harvest.

## Harvest

Pick outer leaves, never more than one-third of the plant at a time. Roughly
**November–March**.
