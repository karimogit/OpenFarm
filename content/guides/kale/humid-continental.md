## Summary

Kale is one of the most reliable leafy crops for short, cool summers. It handles
frost well and keeps producing into late autumn.

## Sowing

- Start indoors in March–April for early plants, or sow directly from about
  **mid-April to early July** (UMN Minnesota). Later sowings give a crop for autumn.

## Planting

Transplant 30–45 cm apart once seedlings have 4–6 true leaves.

## Harvest

Harvest outer leaves from mid-summer until the plants are buried by snow,
typically **July–November**.

## Problems

Flea beetles in spring, cabbage caterpillars in summer. Insect mesh works well.
