## Summary

Kale (Acephala group of *Brassica oleracea*; *boerenkool* in Dutch) is one of the
hardiest leafy vegetables. It stands through winter and is a classic
"hungry gap" crop. Frost tends to improve the flavour.

## Sowing

- Indoors in modules from **March**, or outdoors in a seedbed or in its final
  position from **March to June**, about 1 cm deep.
- Move seedlings to their final site 6–8 weeks after sowing, when 10–15 cm tall.

## Planting

Space plants **30–60 cm** apart depending on variety, rows at least 45 cm apart.
Firm in well – tall plants become top-heavy in winter winds.

## Harvest

From **September** through winter into early spring. Pick a few lower leaves from
each plant at a time.

## Problems

Cabbage white caterpillars, pigeons, whitefly and clubroot. Cover with fine mesh
netting; rotate brassicas.
