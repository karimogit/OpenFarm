## Summary

In hot-summer Mediterranean climates, kale is a **cool-season** crop grown through
the mild, wet winter.

## Sowing and planting

Start in late summer (**August–September**) so plants establish before winter. UC's
Sonoma County summary gives 12 inches (about 30 cm) between plants and 18–24 inches
(45–60 cm) between rows, with 65–75 days to maturity.

## Harvest

**October to April**. Flavour is best in cool weather and becomes sweeter after
light frost.

## Problems

Aphids and cabbage caterpillars; summer heat causes bolting.
