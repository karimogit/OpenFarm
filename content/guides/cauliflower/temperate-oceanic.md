## Summary

Cauliflower is several crops on one calendar. Different types head in different months, which is why the harvest row is marked all year. Sow the type that matches the season you want.

## Sowing and planting

Sow under cover in January–March. Sow outdoors in March–June. Plant in March–August. Between rows, allow 60–70 cm.

## Harvest

The harvest row is marked in every month.

The earliest sowings are under cover. Later ones can go in a seedbed, about 1.5 cm deep. Final rows are 60–70 cm apart. Any check in watering or planting is what makes the heads button instead of filling.
