## Summary

Parsnips are sown once, early, and left to size up. Seed is slow and does not keep well from one year to the next.

## Sowing and planting

Sow outdoors in March–May. In the row, allow 15 cm. Between rows, allow 30 cm.

## Harvest

Harvest in September–March.

Sow about 1 cm deep and thin to 15 cm, with rows about 30 cm apart. Roots can stay in the ground through winter and be lifted when you want them.
