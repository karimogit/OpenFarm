## Summary

Gooseberries are a tough bush fruit for this climate. They can be grown as a bush, a cordon or a fan.

## Sowing and planting

Plant in November–March.

## Harvest

Harvest in June–August.

Plant while dormant. Bushes are spaced about 1.2–1.5 m apart; cordons 30–38 cm. Fruit can be thinned and cooked while green, or left to ripen in early to mid summer. Watch for sawfly larvae stripping the leaves.
