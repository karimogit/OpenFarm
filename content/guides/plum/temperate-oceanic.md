## Summary

Plums, gages and damsons are reliable tree fruit here if blossom escapes frost. Many cultivars are self-fertile.

## Sowing and planting

Plant in November–March.

## Harvest

Harvest in July–October.

Plant dormant trees. A wall-trained tree should sit 25–35 cm out from the masonry. Thin a heavy set so the branches do not break: the RHS thinning is down to one fruit every 5–8 cm, or a pair every 15 cm. Harvest runs from midsummer into autumn depending on the variety.
