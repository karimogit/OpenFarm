## Summary

Blackcurrants fruit on young wood and like a rich, moisture-retentive soil. They are one of the most reliable bush fruits in a cool, wet summer.

## Sowing and planting

Plant in November–March.

## Harvest

Harvest in July–August.

Plant dormant bushes about 5 cm deeper than they stood in the nursery, so they throw new shoots from below ground. The main harvest is mid to late summer. Winter pruning takes out a share of the oldest dark stems.
