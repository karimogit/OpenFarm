## Summary

Figs grow easily and fruit reluctantly in a cool summer unless the roots are restricted. A lined pit or a large pot keeps the plant fruitful instead of all leaf.

## Sowing and planting

Plant in March–April.

## Harvest

Harvest in July–September.

Plant in March or April, in sun, against a warm wall if you can. A rooting pit about 60 cm deep and wide, with the sides lined and a rubble layer in the base, is the usual way to limit roots in the ground. Fruit ripens in late summer where the season is warm enough; a cool year may not finish the crop.
