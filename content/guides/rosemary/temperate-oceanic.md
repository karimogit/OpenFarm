## Summary

Rosemary is an evergreen shrub for a sunny, well-drained place. Wet, cold soil kills more plants than frost alone.

## Sowing and planting

Sow outdoors in March–May. Plant in March–May. In the row, allow 45 cm.

## Harvest

The harvest row is marked in every month.

Plant in spring, at least 45 cm apart, or sow seed then and move plants out once they are about 10 cm tall. Sprigs can be cut in any month once the shrub has some size.
