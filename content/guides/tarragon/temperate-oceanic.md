## Summary

Two tarragons share this crop. French tarragon is the culinary one and is planted, not sown. Russian tarragon is the one that grows easily from seed and survives winters more reliably, with a coarser flavour.

## Sowing and planting

Sow outdoors in March–June. Plant in April–June. In the row, allow 20–30 cm.

## Harvest

Harvest in March–November.

French tarragon is generally not sold as seed. Space Russian plants at least 30 cm apart and the smaller French plants about 20 cm. Pick shoots through the growing season.
