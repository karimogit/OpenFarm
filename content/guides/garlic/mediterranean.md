## Summary

Mediterranean climates suit garlic very well: it grows through the mild, wet winter
and matures as the dry season starts. Most commercial garlic in the USA is grown in
California's mild climate.

## Planting

- UC lists **October–December** for most of California; in the Sacramento Valley the
  best window is **September to mid-December**. Earlier planting tends to give higher
  yields.
- Plant cloves pointed end up, 2.5–5 cm deep, 8–13 cm apart in rows 30–45 cm apart
  (UC Sonoma County summary). Softneck varieties do well here.
- Good drainage matters – winter rain on heavy soil can rot cloves.

## Care

Winter rain usually supplies enough water; irrigate in dry spring spells and stop
watering about a month before harvest.

## Harvest

About 9–10 months after planting, usually **late May to July**, when the lower leaves
brown. Cure in shade with good airflow.
