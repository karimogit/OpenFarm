## Summary

Garlic is one of the easiest crops here: plant in autumn, mulch, and lift the next summer.

## Planting

- Plant from **late September into October**, before about **15 October**, so cloves root before the
  ground freezes. Too early and they sprout before winter; too late and roots are too weak.
- Break bulbs into cloves just before planting, keeping the papery skin on. Plant pointed end up,
  **5–10 cm deep**, **10–15 cm** apart in rows **30 cm** apart, in sunny, well-drained soil with compost.
- Cover with **8–10 cm of mulch** (straw or leaves).

## Care

- Garlic needs steady moisture, including during dry winters. Water about 2.5 cm per week in dry
  spells until mid-June, then stop so bulbs mature.
- In spring, thin the mulch as it warms. Keep beds weed-free.
- Cut off hardneck scapes when they appear to get bigger bulbs.

## Harvest and curing

- Stop watering about two weeks before harvest. Lift when about half the leaves have turned brown.
- Cure for two to three weeks in a warm, shaded, airy place, then store cool (about 4–15 °C).
  Keep some large bulbs for planting in October.
