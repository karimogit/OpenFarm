## Summary

Mulberries become large trees. A tree raised from seed can take about eight years to fruit; named varieties are often quicker.

## Sowing and planting

Plant in October–April.

## Harvest

Harvest in August–September.

Plant from autumn into spring. The trees are self-fertile. Fruit ripens in late summer and the juice stains, so pick ripe fruit individually if you want to keep it off your hands. A pot of about 50 cm is a start for a young tree, not a permanent home.
