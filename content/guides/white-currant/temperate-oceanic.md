## Summary

Whitecurrants are grown like redcurrants. The RHS gives them the same planting season and the same mid to late summer harvest.

## Sowing and planting

Plant in November–March. In the row, allow 30–150 cm.

## Harvest

Harvest in July–August.

Plant dormant bushes about 1.2–1.5 m apart, or cordons 30–40 cm apart. They fruit on old wood, so winter pruning shortens side shoots rather than cutting the bush to the ground.
