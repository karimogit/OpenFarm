## Summary

Hardy kiwi (Actinidia arguta) is the smaller-fruited species on the RHS kiwi page. It is hardier as a plant than the fuzzy kiwi, but the young shoots are just as vulnerable to late frost, and the month grid is not split by species.

## Sowing and planting

Plant in March–May. In the row, allow 300–450 cm.

## Harvest

Harvest in August–September.

Use the same spring planting window, a strong support and 3–4.5 m between plants. Fruit is smaller and smooth-skinned. Do not read the August–September harvest as a promise in a cool year; frost on the new shoots is the first limit.
