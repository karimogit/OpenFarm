## Summary

Oregano is a hardy perennial for a sunny, well-drained spot. Flavour is stronger on a plant that is not overfed or sitting wet.

## Sowing and planting

Sow outdoors in March–May. Plant in May–June. In the row, allow 20–30 cm.

## Harvest

Harvest in May–October.

Sow in spring or plant young plants once the soil has warmed, 20–30 cm apart. Pick shoots from late spring through autumn.
