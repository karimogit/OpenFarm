## Timing

Colorado State University groups this crop as **hardy**: plant as soon as the soil is dry enough, as early as 2–4 weeks before the average last frost (15 May on the Front Range). Typical days to harvest: 120 from transplanting.

The average frost dates on the Front Range are 15 May (last) and 15 October (first) according to CSU PlantTalk; harvest months are estimated and stop at the autumn frost.
