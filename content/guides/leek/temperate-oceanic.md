## Summary

Leeks are the classic winter allium for a mild, wet climate. They stand in the bed from late summer until spring.

## Sowing and planting

Sow outdoors in February–June. Plant in May–July. In the row, allow 15–20 cm. Between rows, allow 30 cm.

## Harvest

Harvest in August–March.

Sow about 1.5 cm deep. Transplant when plants are about pencil thick, into holes 15–20 cm apart and about 30 cm between rows. Drop a plant into each hole and water it in rather than firming the soil hard.
