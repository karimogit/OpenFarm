## Summary

Pumpkins need the whole summer. Bush plants take less room than trailing ones, but both want rich soil and no frost.

## Sowing and planting

Sow under cover in April–May. Plant in May–June. In the row, allow 90–150 cm. Between rows, allow 90–150 cm. Do not put frost-tender plants outside until the frost risk has passed.

## Harvest

Harvest in September–October.

Start seed in pots in April or May and plant out after the last frost. Bush plants need about 90 cm; trailing plants about 1.5 m. Cut fruit before hard frost and cure it somewhere dry if you want it to store.
