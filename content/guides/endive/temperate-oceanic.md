## Summary

Endive is a bitter salad leaf for succession sowings. Full heads need more room than a baby-leaf row.

## Sowing and planting

Sow outdoors in February–August. Plant in April–June. In the row, allow 23–38 cm. Between rows, allow 30 cm.

## Harvest

Harvest in March–November.

Sow about 1 cm deep. Thin full plants to 23–38 cm, or to about 5 cm if you only want baby leaves, with rows about 30 cm apart.
