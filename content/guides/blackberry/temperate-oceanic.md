## Summary

Blackberries and the hybrid berries on the same page are cane fruit for a fence or a post-and-wire row. Modern thornless cultivars make the job kinder.

## Sowing and planting

Plant in October–March.

## Harvest

Harvest in July–September.

Plant while dormant, from autumn to early spring. Set the top of the root ball about 5 cm below the surface. Fruit ripens through late summer. After harvest, cut out the canes that have fruited on summer-fruiting kinds.
