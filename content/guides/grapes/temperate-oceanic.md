## Summary

Outdoor dessert grapes need a warm wall or a greenhouse in this climate. A cool, wet autumn is what usually stops the fruit finishing.

## Sowing and planting

Plant in November–March.

## Harvest

Harvest in August–October.

Plant dormant vines from autumn to early spring, at least 20 cm out from a wall. Train them on wires and prune every winter; an unpruned vine is all leaf. Harvest, when the season allows, is late summer into autumn.
