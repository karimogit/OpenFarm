## Summary

Beetroot is a direct-sown root for repeated small sowings. Pull roots while they are still about 4–7 cm across if you want them tender.

## Sowing and planting

Sow outdoors in March–July. In the row, allow 10 cm. Between rows, allow 30 cm.

## Harvest

Harvest in June–October.

Sow about 2.5 cm deep, with roughly 10 cm between seeds and 30 cm between rows. Each corky seed cluster can give more than one seedling, so thin to one plant.
