## Summary

Sorrel is a hardy perennial leaf with a sharp, lemony taste. It comes early and keeps producing if you stop it flowering.

## Sowing and planting

Sow outdoors in March–June. Plant in March–June.

## Harvest

Harvest in March–November.

Sow about 1 cm deep in spring, or plant divisions then. Cut leaves often. Flower stems take energy from the leaf crop, so remove them unless you want seed.
