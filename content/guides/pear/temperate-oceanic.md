## Summary

Pears suit this climate better than peaches do, but they flower early enough that a frost pocket will lose the crop. Most trees need a pollination partner.

## Sowing and planting

Plant in October–April.

## Harvest

Harvest in August–October.

Plant while dormant, including into April. On a wall, set the tree about 30 cm out from the base. Pick fruit in late summer and autumn, often while it is still firm, and finish ripening it indoors.
