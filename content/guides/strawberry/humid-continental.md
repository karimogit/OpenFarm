## Summary

Winter cold is the main risk: crowns can be killed at about −9 °C (15 °F) without protection, so
straw mulch (and snow) matter. Choose hardy varieties.

## Planting

- Plant dormant bare-root transplants in **April–May** and potted plants in **May–June** after frost.
- Full sun (at least 6, ideally 10+ hours), well-drained soil, beyond the root zone of trees.
- Matted rows: plants **45–60 cm** apart, rows **0.9–1.2 m** apart.
- Pinch off flowers for the first few weeks after planting.

## Care

- About 2.5 cm of water per week, early in the day, ideally with drip or a soaker hose.
- Guide or remove runners through summer.
- Renovate June-bearing beds in the two weeks after harvest (before 1 August): mow, thin rows and
  remove old leaves. Fertilise after renovation, not in spring.
- After two or three hard frosts in late autumn, cover plants with **10–15 cm of straw**; rake it
  into the paths in spring when growth starts.
- Move the bed every three to four years.

## Harvest

June-bearing types: mid-June to early July (about four weeks after flowering). Day-neutral types
fruit through the summer until frost.
