## Summary

Chicory covers forcing types, sugarloaf heads and loose leaves. The same calendar runs from an early sowing through to heads and leaves in summer, autumn and winter.

## Sowing and planting

Sow outdoors in February–October. Plant in May–June. In the row, allow 15–30 cm. Between rows, allow 30 cm.

## Harvest

Harvest in July–March.

Sow about 1 cm deep in rows 30 cm apart. Thin forcing types to about 15 cm and non-forcing heads to about 30 cm. Baby leaves can sit much closer, about 5 cm.
