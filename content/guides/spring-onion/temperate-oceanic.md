## Summary

Salad onions are a succession crop, not a bulb onion. Sow short rows often so you always have plants at pencil size.

## Sowing and planting

Sow outdoors in March–September. Plant in March–May. In the row, allow 2–3 cm. Between rows, allow 20 cm.

## Harvest

Harvest in March–October.

Sow about 1 cm deep in rows 20 cm apart and thin to about 2.5 cm. Closer than that encourages mildew. The harvest runs from spring into autumn if you keep sowing.
