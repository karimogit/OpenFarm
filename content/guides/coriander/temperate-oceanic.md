## Summary

Coriander is sown little and often. It bolts quickly in heat, so a single spring sowing does not last the year.

## Sowing and planting

Sow outdoors in February–October. Plant in April–June. In the row, allow 5–20 cm.

## Harvest

Harvest in April–December.

For leaves, thin to about 5 cm and start cutting when plants are 10–15 cm tall. For seed, give plants about 20 cm. Later sowings extend the leaf harvest into autumn.
