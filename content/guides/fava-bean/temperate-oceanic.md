## Summary

Broad beans are one of the earliest outdoor legumes in a mild winter. A spring sowing and an autumn sowing are both on the calendar.

## Sowing and planting

Sow outdoors in February–May, October–November. Plant in April–May. In the row, allow 15–23 cm. Between rows, allow 45 cm.

## Harvest

Harvest in May–August.

Set seed 5–7.5 cm deep, 15–23 cm apart, with about 45 cm between single rows. October and November sowings stand through a mild winter and crop earlier than the spring sowings. Pinch out the tops once the first pods have set, which also slows blackfly.
