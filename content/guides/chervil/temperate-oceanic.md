## Summary

Chervil is a soft annual herb that prefers cool weather and a little shade. It runs to seed in hot sun.

## Sowing and planting

Sow outdoors in March–August. Plant in April–June. In the row, allow 15 cm. Between rows, allow 30 cm.

## Harvest

Harvest in May–November.

Sow about 1 cm deep, in rows 30 cm apart, and thin to 15 cm. Keep sowing so you have leaves after the first plants flower.
