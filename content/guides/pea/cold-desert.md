## Summary

Peas are the opening cool-season crop in UNR Extension’s Reno calendar. English
peas, snow peas and sugar snap peas (and sweet peas) can be planted on 17 March,
with other cool-season vegetables following about a month later.

## Sowing

- Direct-seed in March, as soon as the early cool-season garden starts.
- The fact sheet’s dates are averages for the Reno area; north valleys can be
  one to two weeks later.
- Full sun for at least six to eight hours and a reliable water supply are the
  general site recommendations; drip irrigation is commonly used in this dry
  climate.

## Harvest

No harvest month is given in the fact sheet. May–June is listed from the
mid-March sowing date only.
