## Summary

The Pima County chart treats shelling, snow and sugar snap peas the same. Sow
seeds are marked in January and from mid-October through December. Harvest is
marked in February, March and April. There is no summer sowing or harvest on the
chart.

## Timing

- Sow seeds: January; mid-October through December.
- Harvest: February–April. The three pea types on the chart share this window.
