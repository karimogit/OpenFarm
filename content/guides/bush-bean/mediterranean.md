## Summary

A warm-season crop; sow directly into warm soil. Two windows avoid the hottest weeks.

## Sowing

- Direct-sow in **April–May**, and again in **July–August** for an autumn crop.
- UC's Sonoma County summary gives 2–4 inches (5–10 cm) between plants, 30 inches
  (about 75 cm) between rows and 48–70 days to maturity for bush beans.

## Care

Irrigate regularly – there is no summer rain. Mulch keeps the soil cooler.

## Harvest

Pick young pods before the seeds bulge, **June to October**.
