## Summary

This guide follows the Pima County row for snap (bush) beans, not the separate
pole, lima or cowpea rows. Sow seeds are marked in April–early May and again in
August. Harvest is marked in June–early July and in November–December.

## Timing

- Sow seeds: April and early May; August.
- Harvest: June–early July; November–December.
