## Summary

Fast-maturing (45–60 days) and well suited to the cooler half of the year in the
wet–dry tropics.

## Sowing

UF/IFAS (South Florida): sow **September–April**, 1–1.5 inches (2.5–4 cm) deep,
2–4 inches (5–10 cm) apart in rows 18 inches (45 cm) apart. Fertilise at half the
rate used for other vegetables – too much nitrogen limits production.

## Harvest

Pick pods young and often, roughly **November–June** depending on sowing date.
