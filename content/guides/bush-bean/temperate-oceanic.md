## Summary

Frost-tender and fast. Dwarf (bush) types need no tall supports and suit small
gardens and containers. Like other legumes they fix nitrogen with root bacteria.

## Sowing

- Indoors from **late April** for an early crop, one seed per pot, 5 cm deep.
- Outdoors once all risk of frost has passed and the soil is warming – usually
  **late May or early June** – 5 cm deep and **15 cm** apart in blocks or double rows.
- Make several sowings until mid-summer for a longer harvest.

## Planting

Harden off indoor-raised plants and plant out after the last frost, 15 cm apart.

## Care

Water well in dry weather, especially once flowering starts.

## Harvest

Pick pods at 15–20 cm, when they snap easily and before the beans show through –
roughly **July to October**. Regular picking keeps plants cropping.
