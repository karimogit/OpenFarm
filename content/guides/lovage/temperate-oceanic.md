## Summary

Lovage is a tall perennial herb with a strong celery flavour. One plant is usually enough.

## Sowing and planting

Sow outdoors in March–May. Plant in May–June. In the row, allow 30–45 cm.

## Harvest

Harvest in April–October, December–February.

Allow 30–45 cm at first, and more once the clump is mature. Young leaves are the part to pick; the flavour gets harsh as stems age.
