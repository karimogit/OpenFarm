## Summary

Redcurrants fruit on older wood and take a shaded spot better than most fruit. A bush or a cordon both work.

## Sowing and planting

Plant in November–March. In the row, allow 30–150 cm.

## Harvest

Harvest in July–August.

Plant while dormant. Bushes need about 1.2–1.5 m; cordons 30–40 cm. Strings of fruit ripen in mid to late summer and keep on the bush for a while if birds are kept off.
