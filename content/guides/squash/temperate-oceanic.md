## Summary

The squash calendar covers summer squash, picked young, and winter squash, left to ripen. Both are frost-tender and want rich soil.

## Sowing and planting

Sow under cover in April–May. Plant in May–June. In the row, allow 90–150 cm. Between rows, allow 90–150 cm. Do not put frost-tender plants outside until the frost risk has passed.

## Harvest

Harvest in July–October.

Start seed in pots in April or May and plant after frost. Bush plants need about 90 cm; trailing plants about 1.5 m. Summer fruit is cut through the summer; storage fruit stays on the plant into autumn and is cut before a hard frost.
