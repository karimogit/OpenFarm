## Summary

Potatoes are one of the few staple crops that reliably mature in the far north,
helped by very long summer days. The season is short, so everything is about
gaining time.

## Pre-sprouting

Pre-sprout seed tubers for 4–6 weeks in a light, cool room (about 10–15 °C) so
they carry short, sturdy shoots at planting.

## Planting

- Plant when the soil is about 8 °C, usually **late May to mid-June**.
- Choose **very early varieties**; northern heritage varieties are also grown locally.
- Fleece can protect against late night frosts.

## Harvest

**August–September**, before hard frost. Early varieties can give new potatoes
from late July in good years.
