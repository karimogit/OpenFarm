## Summary

Potatoes are a cool-season crop in hot-summer climates. Plant in late winter for
a crop before the summer heat, and optionally again in late summer for an autumn crop.

## Planting

- Spring crop: plant when the soil reaches about 13 °C (55 °F) – in the Sacramento
  area usually **late February to early March**.
- Autumn crop: plant in **August** with irrigation.
- Use certified seed potatoes; potatoes prefer loose, well-drained soil and
  dislike heavy clay, so raised beds work well.

## Harvest

Most varieties take 110–140 days in Sacramento (UC), less for early varieties.
Lift the spring crop around **May–July** when tops die back; the autumn crop
around **November–December**.

## Problems

Heat and drought stress, and tuber moth in hot, dry conditions – keep tubers well
covered with soil.
