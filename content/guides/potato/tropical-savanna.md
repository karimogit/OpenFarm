## Summary

In warm lowlands, potatoes are a **cool-season** crop. UF/IFAS lists planting from
**October to January** in South Florida, with 85–110 days to harvest.

## Planting

- Plant 2-ounce (about 55 g) **certified** seed pieces with at least one eye, 3–4
  inches (8–10 cm) deep. Don't use supermarket potatoes, which may carry disease or
  sprout inhibitors.
- Spacing (UF/IFAS): 5–10 inches (13–25 cm) between plants, rows 36–42 inches
  (90–105 cm) apart.

## Care

Potatoes need consistent moisture and generous fertiliser. Hill soil around the
stems to keep tubers covered.

## Harvest

About **January to April**, when the foliage yellows and dies back.
