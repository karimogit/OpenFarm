## Summary

Potatoes are a traditional staple here. Soil temperature, not the calendar,
decides when to plant: wait until the soil is about **8 °C** at planting depth.

## Pre-sprouting (förgroning)

Pre-sprout seed tubers in a light, cool room for 4–6 weeks before planting. This
shortens the time in the field by 1–2 weeks – useful because late blight often
arrives in late summer.

## Planting

- Use certified seed potatoes (*certifierat utsäde*).
- Mid-Sweden: usually the **first or second week of May**; southern Sweden earlier
  (April), further north about a week later. Minnesota: late April to mid-May.
- Furrows 8–10 cm deep, rows 50–60 cm apart, tubers 20–30 cm apart.

## Earthing up

Hill soil around the stems as they grow so no tubers are exposed to light.

## Harvest

New potatoes about **7–8 weeks** after planting (July). Main harvest when the
foliage has died back, **August–September**.

## Problems

Late blight (*potatisbladmögel*), scab, wireworms in former grassland. Rotate on
a four-year cycle or longer.
