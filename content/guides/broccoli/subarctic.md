## Timing

University of Alaska Fairbanks (Georgeson Botanical Garden, Fairbanks): Sow indoors about 23 April; plant out 20–25 May (cool-season).

The UAF Extension calendar for Interior Alaska plants directly from 1 June, transplants from 4 June and starts the harvest on 1 August. Harvest months are approximate; quick salad crops may be ready earlier.
