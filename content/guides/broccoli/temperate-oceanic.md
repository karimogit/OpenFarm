## Summary

The broccoli calendar covers two crops: summer heads, and sprouting types that stand through winter and cut in late winter and early spring.

## Sowing and planting

Sow under cover in February–March. Sow outdoors in March–July. Plant in April–August. In the row, allow 60–80 cm.

## Harvest

Harvest in February–March, June–November.

Sow about 2 cm deep. Move plants on when they are 10–15 cm tall, and leave 60–80 cm between them, because the plants get large. Net them against pigeons and caterpillars.
