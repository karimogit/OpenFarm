## Summary

Thyme is a low evergreen for sun and sharp drainage. It hates sitting wet in winter.

## Sowing and planting

Sow outdoors in March–May. Plant in April–June. In the row, allow 20–30 cm.

## Harvest

The harvest row is marked in every month.

Sow in spring or plant young plants after the soil has warmed, 20–30 cm apart. Sprigs can be cut in any month. Trim after flowering so the plant stays dense.
