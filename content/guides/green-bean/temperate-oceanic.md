## Summary

French beans, both dwarf and climbing, are frost-tender. Wait for warm soil before seed goes outdoors.

## Sowing and planting

Sow under cover in April–May. Sow outdoors in May–July. Plant in May–June. In the row, allow 15 cm. Between rows, allow 45 cm. Do not put frost-tender plants outside until the frost risk has passed.

## Harvest

Harvest in July–October.

Sow about 5 cm deep. Dwarf plants can sit 15 cm apart in a block. Climbing plants want a cane every 15 cm, with rows at least 45 cm apart. Pick pods young so the plants keep flowering.
