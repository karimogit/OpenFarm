## Summary

This guide follows the chart row for traditional cucumber, not the separate
Armenian cucumber row (which has a longer transplant and harvest pattern). Sow
or transplant is marked in April–May and again in August. Harvest is marked in
June–July and in October–early November, with a gap through late summer.

## Timing

- Sow or transplant: April–May; August.
- Harvest: June–July; October and early November.
