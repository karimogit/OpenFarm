## Summary

Cucumbers are frost-tender. In a cool oceanic climate they crop most reliably in
a greenhouse, polytunnel or large cold frame; outdoor (ridge) varieties need a
warm, sunny, sheltered spot. The Royal Horticultural Society treats indoor and
outdoor varieties as different classes with different sowing dates.

## Sowing and planting

- Heated greenhouse: sow mid-February to mid-March; plant from late March once
  the house stays above about 12 °C.
- Unheated greenhouse or polytunnel: sow in April; plant from late May.
- Outdoor crops: sow indoors in late April, or direct in late May or early June
  in milder areas; plant out from early June once temperatures stay above about
  12 °C.
- Sow one seed 1–2 cm deep per small pot, and space plants about 30 cm apart.

## Harvest

Under glass, RHS describes a season from mid-summer to mid-autumn; outdoors the
season is shorter and depends on the weather. Pick regularly to keep the plants
fruiting. Fruit size depends on the variety; pick them firm and uniformly green.
