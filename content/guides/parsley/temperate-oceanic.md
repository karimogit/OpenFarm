## Summary

Parsley is a biennial, usually sown every year because the second-year plant runs to flower. Seed is slow to come up.

## Sowing and planting

Sow outdoors in March–August. Plant in April–June. In the row, allow 15–30 cm. Between rows, allow 20–30 cm.

## Harvest

Harvest in May–November.

Sow about 1 cm deep from spring into summer. Space plants 15–30 cm apart, with 20–30 cm between rows. Leaves can be picked until hard weather, and a mild winter often keeps a plant going.
