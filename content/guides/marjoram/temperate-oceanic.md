## Summary

Sweet marjoram is less hardy than oregano. Treat a young plant as a summer herb unless you can overwinter it somewhere dry and frost-free.

## Sowing and planting

Sow outdoors in March–May. Plant in May–July. In the row, allow 20 cm.

## Harvest

Harvest in May–October.

Sow in spring and plant out after frost, about 20 cm from its neighbours. Pick shoots through summer, before the plant flowers if you want the mildest leaf.
