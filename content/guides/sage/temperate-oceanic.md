## Summary

Sage is a woody perennial for sun and drainage. Older bushes get leggy; a spring trim after flowering keeps them leafy.

## Sowing and planting

Sow outdoors in March–May. Plant in March–May. In the row, allow 30 cm.

## Harvest

The harvest row is marked in every month.

Plant or sow in spring and leave at least 30 cm around the plant. Leaves can be picked year-round. A waterlogged winter is the usual cause of loss.
