## Summary

Asparagus is a long-lived bed crop for a sheltered spot with soil that drains freely. Most people plant dormant crowns; a bed grown from seed takes longer before the first cut.

## Sowing and planting

Sow under cover in February. Sow outdoors in March–April. Plant in March. In the row, allow 30–45 cm. Between rows, allow 45 cm.

## Harvest

Harvest in April–June.

Give crowns two years before you cut any spears, and three years if you raised the plants from seed. In the first cutting year, stop after about six weeks from mid-April; after that, an eight-week cut is the usual season. When the fern turns yellow, cut it to the ground.
