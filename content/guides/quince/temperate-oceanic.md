## Summary

Quinces are a small, ornamental fruit tree that likes a warm summer to finish the fruit. They are more forgiving than apricots, and the fruit is cooked rather than eaten off the tree.

## Sowing and planting

Plant in November–March.

## Harvest

Harvest in October–November.

Plant while dormant, in deep, fertile soil that does not dry out. Harvest in October and November. Fruit is hard when picked and is used cooked.
