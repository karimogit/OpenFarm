## Summary

Rocket is a fast salad leaf. In heat it runs to flower, so keep sowing and pick leaves young.

## Sowing and planting

Sow outdoors in February–September. In the row, allow 15 cm.

## Harvest

Harvest in April–December.

Sow about 1 cm deep from late winter into early autumn and thin to about 15 cm. In hot weather the plants run to flower and the leaves get hotter, so keep sowing.
