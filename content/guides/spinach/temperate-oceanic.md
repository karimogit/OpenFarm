## Summary

Spinach is a cool-season leaf. Spring and late-summer sowings fit an oceanic year better than midsummer.

## Sowing and planting

Sow outdoors in March–May, August–September. In the row, allow 8–15 cm. Between rows, allow 20 cm.

## Harvest

Harvest in March–November.

Sow about 2.5 cm deep in rows 20 cm apart. Thin to at least 15 cm, or closer if you only cut baby leaves. Keep the soil moist so plants do not bolt.
