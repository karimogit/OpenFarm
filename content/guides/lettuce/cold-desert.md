## Summary

UNR Extension treats lettuce as a cool-season crop for the Reno area. After an
early mid-March sowing of peas and spinach, most other cool-season crops,
including lettuce, can be direct-seeded until the end of May. A second cool
season starts in August; many of those plantings can be harvested well into
autumn and will overwinter if protected.

## Sowing

- Direct-seed lettuce from April through the end of May in the Reno area.
- Sow again starting in August for autumn harvest.
- Full sun for at least six to eight hours is the general site advice; the fact
  sheet also notes drip irrigation as a common way to water in this dry climate.

## Harvest

Harvest months are not tabulated in the fact sheet. Spring sowings are listed as
June–July and the August sowing as September–October, from those planting windows
and the note that the second season can be picked well into autumn.
