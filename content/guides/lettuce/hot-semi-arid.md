## Summary

Lettuce is a cool-season crop on the Pima County chart. Sow or transplant is
marked through January and February, and again from October through December.
Harvest is marked only in March, April and early May. The chart does not mark a
summer crop, and it does not mark harvest in the autumn sowing months themselves
— autumn plantings line up with the following spring harvest window.

## Timing

- Sow or transplant: January–February; October–December.
- Harvest: March, April and early May.
