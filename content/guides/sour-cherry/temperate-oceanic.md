## Summary

Sour cherries, such as Morello, are on the same RHS cherry calendar as the sweet kinds. They are the more reliable outdoor cherry in a cool, cloudy summer and are often self-fertile.

## Sowing and planting

Plant in November–March.

## Harvest

Harvest in June–August.

Plant dormant trees from autumn to early spring. They also crop in partial shade, which is why a north-facing fan is a traditional spot for Morello. Harvest is early to mid summer. Net the tree if birds can reach it.
