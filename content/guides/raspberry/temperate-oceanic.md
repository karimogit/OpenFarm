## Summary

Raspberries are a cane fruit for a post-and-wire row. Summer-fruiting and autumn-fruiting kinds are pruned differently, so keep them apart.

## Sowing and planting

Plant in November–March. In the row, allow 40–60 cm. Between rows, allow 150–200 cm.

## Harvest

Harvest in June–October.

Plant dormant canes 40–60 cm apart, with 1.5–2 m between rows, and no more than about 5 cm of soil over the top roots. Summer kinds fruit on last year's canes; autumn kinds fruit on the current year's canes and can be cut to the ground in winter. The harvest row runs from early summer into autumn because both types are included.
