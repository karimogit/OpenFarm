## Summary

Swedes are a maincrop root for autumn and winter, sown later than turnips so they size up in the cool part of the year.

## Sowing and planting

Sow outdoors in April–June. Plant in June. In the row, allow 20–25 cm. Between rows, allow 38 cm.

## Harvest

Harvest in September–December.

Sow about 2 cm deep in rows 38 cm apart and thin to 20–25 cm. For baby roots, 8–10 cm is enough. They are not a midsummer salad crop; the harvest is autumn into winter.
