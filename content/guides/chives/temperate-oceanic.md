## Summary

Chives are a perennial clump. A spring sowing or a bought plant both settle in the same season.

## Sowing and planting

Sow outdoors in March–May. Plant in April–June.

## Harvest

Harvest in May–November.

Leaves can be cut from late spring until they die back. Leave some growth on the plant so it can recover. Flowers are edible too, but remove them if you want more leaf.
