## Summary

Salad radishes are a short crop for succession sowing. Winter radishes and pods sit on the same calendar and take longer.

## Sowing and planting

Sow outdoors in March–September.

## Harvest

Harvest in April–February.

Sow little and often. Summer radishes are ready in a few weeks; the winter types and the edible seed pods occupy the colder months on the harvest row. Thin so roots are not crowded.
