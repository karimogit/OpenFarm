## Summary

Celeriac is a slow root celery. It is started under cover and planted into soil that does not dry out.

## Sowing and planting

Sow under cover in March–April. Plant in May–June. In the row, allow 30 cm. Between rows, allow 40–45 cm.

## Harvest

Harvest in October–March.

Space plants 30 cm apart, with 40–45 cm between rows. The swollen base can stay in the ground into winter and be lifted as needed.
