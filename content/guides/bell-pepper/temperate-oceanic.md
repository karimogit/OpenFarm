## Summary

Bell peppers are the sweet, blocky pepper. They are grown like other sweet peppers: a long start under cover, then a warm, frost-free spot.

## Sowing and planting

Sow under cover in February–April. Plant in April–June. In the row, allow 38–45 cm. Do not put frost-tender plants outside until the frost risk has passed.

## Harvest

Harvest in July–October.

Do not put plants outside until frost has passed. An April move is only for a heated or very warm greenhouse. Leave 38–45 cm between plants.
