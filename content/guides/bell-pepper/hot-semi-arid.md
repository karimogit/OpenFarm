## Summary

Bell peppers on the Pima County chart are sown in January–early February and
again in June–early July, and transplanted from mid-February through April and
from mid-July through mid-August. The only harvest cells marked are in October
and early November. The chart does not mark a late-spring or early-summer
harvest for this row.

## Timing

- Sow seeds: January, early February; June and early July.
- Transplant: mid-February through April; mid-July through mid-August.
- Harvest: October and early November only, as marked on the January 2025 chart.
The chart does not say whether winter “Sow Seeds” cells are outdoor sowings or
starts under cover.
