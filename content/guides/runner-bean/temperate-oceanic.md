## Summary

Runner beans want a sunny, sheltered row and something tall to climb. They will not tolerate frost.

## Sowing and planting

Sow under cover in April–May. Sow outdoors in May–July. Plant in May–July. In the row, allow 15–30 cm. Between rows, allow 45–60 cm. Do not put frost-tender plants outside until the frost risk has passed.

## Harvest

Harvest in July–October.

Put up canes or a wigwam before you sow: 15–30 cm between plants and 45–60 cm between the two sides of a double row. Sow about 5 cm deep once the soil is warm, or start pots under cover. Pick pods before the seeds swell.
