## Summary

Fuzzy kiwi (Actinidia deliciosa) is a huge climber. Fruit rarely finishes ripening outdoors here except after a long hot summer, and young shoots of both this species and the hardy kiwi are easily cut by a late frost.

## Sowing and planting

Plant in March–May. In the row, allow 300–450 cm.

## Harvest

Harvest in August–September.

Plant in spring on a warm wall or a strong fence, 3–4.5 m apart, in fertile soil that is slightly acid. You need a female or a self-fertile plant if you want fruit. Prune twice a year or the vine buries its support.
