## Summary

This is herb fennel, grown for feathery leaves and seed, not the swollen bulb of Florence fennel. It becomes a tall perennial clump.

## Sowing and planting

Sow outdoors in April–May. Plant in May–June. In the row, allow 30 cm.

## Harvest

Harvest in June–October.

Sow about 1 cm deep and thin to at least 30 cm. Leaves can be cut through the summer; seed follows the flowers.
