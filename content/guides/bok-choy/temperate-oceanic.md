## Summary

Pak choi is a quick brassica leaf. It bolts in hot, dry spells, so the useful sowings in a cool summer are the later ones as much as the first.

## Sowing and planting

Sow outdoors in April–August. Plant in May. In the row, allow 8–30 cm. Between rows, allow 30 cm.

## Harvest

Harvest in May–November.

Sow about 2 cm deep in rows at least 30 cm apart. Thin to 8–10 cm for baby leaves, about 20 cm for a loose plant, and 25–30 cm for a full head.
