The black cherry is a large native North American tree bearing small, dark, astringent fruit used for preserves, wine, and flavoring. Foragers and wildlife prize the fruit, and the wood is a premium furniture timber. Note that its leaves and pits contain cyanide compounds and are toxic to livestock.
