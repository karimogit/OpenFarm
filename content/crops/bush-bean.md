Bush beans are compact forms of the common bean grown for their young pods (green, yellow "wax"
or purple) or for dry beans. They need warm soil, are killed by frost and do not need support.
Climbing (pole) beans are the same species with a vining habit.

Heirloom bean varieties are open-pollinated, so seed can be saved and replanted.
