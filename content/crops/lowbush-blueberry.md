The lowbush blueberry is a low, spreading perennial shrub native to northeastern North America, producing small, intensely sweet berries. Cold-hardy and rhizomatous, it forms dense mats and thrives in acidic, well-drained soil. This is the wild blueberry of commerce, more often managed than planted.
