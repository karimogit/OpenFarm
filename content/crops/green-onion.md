Green onions have a small bulb and tall, hollow, tubular leaves. The leaves and bulbs can be eaten raw or cooked in salads, stews, soups, and other dishes. They have a milder taste than most onions. Green onions are members of the Allium family and are related to garlic, shallots, leeks, and chives.
