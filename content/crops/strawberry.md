Garden strawberries are low perennial plants grown for their fruit. They grow in beds, borders,
containers and even hanging baskets, and spread by runners.

## Types

- **Summer-fruiting / June-bearing**: one large crop over two to three weeks in early summer.
- **Perpetual / ever-bearing**: smaller flushes from early summer into autumn.
- **Day-neutral**: keep flowering and fruiting through most of the season.
- **Alpine (*Fragaria vesca*)**: small aromatic berries, tolerates light shade, often grown from seed.
