Kai-lan, or Chinese broccoli, is a leafy brassica grown for its thick, crisp stems, glossy blue-green leaves, and small flower buds. It has a mild, sweet-bitter flavor and is a staple of Cantonese stir-fries, often blanched and served with oyster sauce. It grows quickly in cool weather.
