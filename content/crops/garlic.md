Garlic forms a bulb made of cloves. It is usually planted in autumn so the cloves root before the
ground freezes, then grows on in spring and is lifted in summer when part of the foliage has browned.

There are two main groups: **hardneck** garlic, which sends up a flower stalk (scape) that is
usually removed so the plant puts energy into the bulb, and **softneck** garlic, which usually
does not. Bulbs are cured in a dry, shaded, airy place before storing.
