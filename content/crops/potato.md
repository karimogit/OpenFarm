Potatoes are cool-season tuber crops in the nightshade family, originally from South America.
They are planted from seed tubers and grow best in loose soil rich in organic matter. Only the
tubers are eaten: leaves, fruit and green tubers are poisonous.

Soil is mounded ("hilled") around the stems or covered with straw so the developing tubers stay
dark and do not turn green. Late blight is the most serious disease.
