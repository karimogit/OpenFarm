Anise hyssop is an upright perennial herb with soft licorice-scented leaves and spikes of purple flowers that bees adore. The leaves and flowers make a sweet herbal tea and season baked goods and salads. Easy to grow and drought-tolerant, it is one of the best pollinator plants for the homestead garden.
