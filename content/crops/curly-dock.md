Curly dock is a hardy perennial wild green with tangy, lemony young leaves eaten cooked or raw in small amounts. A common forageable across much of the world, it also throws up rust-colored seed stalks used in dried arrangements. Older leaves turn bitter and high in oxalic acid, so tender young growth is preferred.
