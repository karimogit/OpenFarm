Groundnut, or hopniss, is a native North American perennial vine that produces strings of edible, protein-rich tubers along its roots. A traditional Indigenous food and a permaculture favorite, it fixes nitrogen and thrives in moist soils and along pond edges. Tubers are dug in fall and cooked like potatoes.
