Adjuma, Aji Umba, or Adjoema peppers are a variety of Capsicum chinense chili pepper originally from Suriname. The peppers are similar in heat, aroma, and flavor to Habanero, but have a boxier shape. The peppers ripen from lime-green to red or yellow on the plant. They are often mistaken for Habanero or Scotch Bonnets.
