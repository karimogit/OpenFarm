Wax beans are a type of snap bean with vibrant yellow, patterned, or dark purple skin and a slightly waxier texture. They can be eaten whole with pod and seeds and have a mild and fresh taste. Wax beans come in pole and bush varieties. Like other legumes, wax beans benefit from an inoculant.
