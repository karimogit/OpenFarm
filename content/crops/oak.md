Oak is a genus of approximately 600 species in the beech family, Fagaceae. Oaks are grown for their strong, hard woods and their acorns, which are edible after the tannins are leached out. Oaks can be shrubs or trees as tall as 24 m. More growing information is available in individual species entries.
