Apples are deciduous fruit trees in the rose family and one of the easiest tree fruits to grow.
Thousands of varieties exist – eating (dessert), cooking and dual-purpose – with different
flavours, harvest times, storage life and disease resistance.

## Choosing a tree

1. **Pollination**: plant two compatible varieties that flower at the same time, or a self-fertile one.
2. **Rootstock**: decides how big the tree gets (see quick facts).
3. **Winter chill**: in mild-winter climates pick a low-chill variety, or the tree will flower and
   leaf out poorly.

Bare-root trees are planted while dormant; container trees can go in at most times of year.
