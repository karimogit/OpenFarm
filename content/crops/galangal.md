Galangal is a tropical rhizome in the ginger family, sharper and more citrusy than common ginger. It is a foundational aromatic in Thai, Indonesian, and Malaysian cooking, used fresh in curry pastes and soups. Like ginger, it grows from rhizome pieces and needs warmth, humidity, and a long growing season.
