Feverfew is a bushy short-lived perennial herb with small daisy-like flowers, used traditionally to prevent migraines and reduce fever. It self-seeds freely and grows easily in full sun to part shade. The bitter, aromatic leaves are the medicinal part, taken fresh or dried.
