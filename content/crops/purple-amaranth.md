Purple amaranth is a fast-growing annual grown both as a leafy vegetable and a protein-rich pseudo-grain. Young leaves are cooked like spinach, while the tall seed heads yield tiny, nutritious grains. Heat-loving and drought-tolerant, it is among the most productive crops in hot climates.
