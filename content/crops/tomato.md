Tomatoes are warm-season, frost-tender plants grown for their fruit. They are perennial in their
native range but grown as annuals almost everywhere. Depending on the cultivar it takes roughly
52–90 days from transplanting to the first harvest.

## Growth habit

- **Determinate** plants grow to a set size, set most of their fruit at once and then decline.
  Many early cultivars are determinate.
- **Indeterminate** plants keep growing and fruiting until frost. Most heirloom cultivars are
  indeterminate and need staking, caging or trellising.

## Fruit types

Cherry and grape types (small and sweet), beefsteak (large slicing fruit) and Roma or plum/paste
types (meaty, few seeds, for cooking and canning). Look at the [varieties](varieties/) to compare
days to harvest, habit and disease resistance.
