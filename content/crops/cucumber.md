Cucumbers are warm-season, frost-tender vines grown for their fruit. They are sown directly
once the soil is warm (or started in cell packs a few weeks before planting out) and can be
trellised to save space. Days to harvest are about 50–60 (Clemson), 55 (CSU) and 40–65 (UF/IFAS).
