The lemon is a small evergreen citrus tree bearing tart, aromatic yellow fruit used for juice, zest, and preserving. In mild climates trees flower and fruit nearly year-round; in colder zones they grow well in containers brought indoors for winter. They want full sun, well-drained soil, and protection from frost.
