Komatsuna, or Japanese mustard spinach, is a fast-growing leafy brassica harvested for its mild, tender greens. It shrugs off both heat and frost better than true spinach, making it a productive cut-and-come-again crop across much of the year. Sow directly and harvest leaves young or let plants mature.
