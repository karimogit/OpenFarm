Kale is a leafy form of *Brassica oleracea* – the same species as cabbage, broccoli and cauliflower –
whose leaves grow from upright stems without forming a head. It is a hardy cool-season crop
picked leaf by leaf over a long season.

Curly, flat-leaved and "Tuscan" (lacinato) types are common. Red Russian kale belongs to
*Brassica napus*.
