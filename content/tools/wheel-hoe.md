## What it's for

A wheel hoe is a hoe blade mounted behind a wheel and pushed with two handles. It weeds and cultivates between rows faster than a hand hoe.

## How to use it

Push it along the paths between rows with short strokes so the blade slices just under the surface. It works best in loose, not-too-wet soil and on small weeds.

## Care and maintenance

Clean soil off the blade and wheel after use, keep the blade sharp with a file, oil metal parts and the wheel bearing, and store it dry.
