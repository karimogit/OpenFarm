## What it's for

A spade is a digging tool with a long handle and a blade that is narrower and flatter than a shovel's. It is used to dig planting holes, cut straight edges and turn or lift soil.

## How to use it

Push the blade in vertically with your foot on the tread, lever the handle back to loosen the soil and lift. Keep loads small to spare your back.

## Care and maintenance

Scrape off soil after use, wash and dry it, and wipe the metal with an oily rag. Sharpen the edge with a mill file, keeping the original angle, and treat a wooden handle with linseed oil. Store it dry and off the ground.
