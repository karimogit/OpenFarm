## What it's for

A garden fork (digging fork) has a short handle and four sturdy, usually square tines. It loosens and turns soil, lifts root crops and breaks up clods and compost, and is often easier than a spade in heavy or stony ground.

## How to use it

Drive the tines in with your foot, then lever back to loosen the soil. Use it to lift potatoes and other roots with less slicing than a spade, and to mix in compost. Keep the tines nearly vertical so they do not bend.

## Care and maintenance

Clean soil off after use, dry the tool and store it under cover. Sharpen or straighten tines if they splay, and oil metal and wooden parts before winter (Iowa State University Extension; University of Wisconsin–Madison Extension).
