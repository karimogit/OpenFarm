## What it's for

A broadfork (U-fork, grelinette) is a wide fork with long tines and two handles. It loosens compacted soil and improves aeration and drainage without turning it, so soil structure is kept and buried weed seeds stay buried – it is used in no-till and low-till beds.

## How to use it

Push the tines in with your feet, pull the handles back to crack the soil, lift out and step back a short distance before the next bite. The soil is loosened, not inverted.

## Care and maintenance

Clean the tines after use, remove rust with steel wool and oil the metal; straighten bent tines. Treat wooden handles with linseed oil and store dry.
