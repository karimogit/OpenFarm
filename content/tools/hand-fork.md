## What it's for

A hand fork (hand cultivator) is a short-handled fork with three or four tines for loosening soil and lifting weeds in beds and among established plants, where a long hoe will not fit.

## How to use it

Work the tines just under the soil surface to loosen weeds and lift them roots-and-all. Use it to break a crust before sowing or to open a small planting pocket. Avoid stabbing shallow-rooted crops.

## Care and maintenance

Clean and dry after use; straighten bent tines and oil metal and wood before winter (Iowa State University Extension; University of Wisconsin–Madison Extension).
