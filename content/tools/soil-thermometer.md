## What it's for

A soil thermometer measures temperature in the seedbed or potting mix. Many vegetables germinate poorly in cold soil; a thermometer is a more reliable guide than air temperature alone (University of Minnesota Extension; Iowa State University Extension).

## How to use it

Insert the probe to seed or root depth, wait until the reading settles, and take it in more than one spot. Morning readings are a cautious guide for sowing warm-season crops.

## Care and maintenance

Wipe the probe clean and dry, and store it in its case. Do not bend a stem thermometer or use it as a dibber.
