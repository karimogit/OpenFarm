## What it's for

A bulb planter is a cylindrical or tapered corer that lifts a plug of soil so you can set a bulb or large clove at a consistent depth, then replace the plug. Long-handled versions are used standing up.

## How to use it

Push the planter to the depth marked for the bulb, lift out the core, sit the bulb with the nose up, and replace the soil. In very loose soil a dibber may be simpler; in turf a planter keeps the hole neat.

## Care and maintenance

Empty and dry the cylinder after use so soil does not cake inside, and oil metal parts before storage (Iowa State University Extension).
