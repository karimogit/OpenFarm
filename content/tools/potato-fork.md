## What it's for

A potato fork is a digging fork with broad, often flat tines spaced to lift tubers with less piercing than a narrow-tined fork or a spade.

## How to use it

Start a little way out from the stem, drive the tines under the row, and lever the soil and tubers up. Work along the row and pick by hand so you do not spear the crop. The same tool can lift other shallow roots in loose soil.

## Care and maintenance

Clean and dry after harvest, and oil metal and wood before storage (Iowa State University Extension).
