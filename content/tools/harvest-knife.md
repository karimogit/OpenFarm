## What it's for

A sharp harvest or garden knife (including a simple field knife or serrated tomato knife) cuts fruit, leaves and stems cleanly so you do not tear plants or bruise the crop.

## How to use it

Cut rather than pull: slice the stem or fruit stalk and place the harvest in a trug or basket. Keep fingers clear, and use secateurs instead for woody stems.

## Care and maintenance

Wash plant sap off, dry the blade, and sharpen it. Store sheathed and out of reach of children (Oregon State University Extension).
