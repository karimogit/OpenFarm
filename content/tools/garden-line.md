## What it's for

A garden line is a string stretched between two pins (or a reel and pins) for marking straight rows and bed edges so sowing, planting and hoeing stay aligned (see also garden planning guidance from Clemson Cooperative Extension).

## How to use it

Set a pin at each end of the row, pull the line taut just above the soil, and sow or plant along it. Reel it up when you move to the next row so you do not trip on it.

## Care and maintenance

Dry the line if it is wet, wind it onto the reel, and replace frayed string.
