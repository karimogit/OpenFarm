## What it's for

Loppers are two-handed pruners with long handles for cutting branches thicker than secateurs can manage, typically up to about 3–5 cm depending on the tool. Bypass loppers make a clean cut; anvil loppers are better on dead wood.

## How to use it

Hold the branch near the blade, cut just outside the branch collar, and do not twist the handles. If the wood is too thick, use a pruning saw instead of forcing the loppers, which can wrench the blades.

## Care and maintenance

Wipe sap off the blades, sharpen bypass blades, and oil the pivot. Store dry (Oregon State University Extension; University of Wisconsin–Madison Extension).
