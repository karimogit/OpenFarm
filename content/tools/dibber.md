## What it's for

A dibber (dibble) is a pointed stick for making holes in the ground to plant seeds, seedlings or small bulbs. It comes straight, T-handled, L-shaped or as a trowel-dibber.

## How to use it

Push it into prepared soil to the planting depth, drop in the seed, seedling or bulb and firm the soil back around it. It is also handy for pricking out seedlings and planting leeks.

## Care and maintenance

Wipe or wash it after use and let it dry; oil a wooden dibber now and then to keep it from cracking.
