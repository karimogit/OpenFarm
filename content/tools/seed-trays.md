## What it's for

Seed trays (flats) and cell flats hold seed-starting mix for raising seedlings. Divided cells with one seedling each are better than one open tray, because roots don't tangle and are less damaged at transplanting. All containers need drainage holes.

## How to use it

Fill with moist seed-starting mix, sow, and cover to one to two times the seed's diameter (light-needing and very fine seed on the surface). Water from below by standing the tray in water, or with a fine mist, and drain excess water.

## Care and maintenance

Most plastic trays are reusable: remove soil, wash in soapy water, then disinfect (for example 1 part bleach to 9 parts water, about 30 minutes), rinse and let dry before use.
