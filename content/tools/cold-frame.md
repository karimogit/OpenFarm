## What it's for

A cold frame is a low, unheated box with a transparent lid for hardening off seedlings, starting hardy crops early, and sheltering plants from wind and light frost (University of Minnesota Extension).

## How to use it

Place it in a sheltered, light spot, ideally facing the sun. Open or prop the lid on mild and sunny days to prevent overheating, and close it at night when frost is expected. Water in the morning so leaves can dry.

## Care and maintenance

Clean the glazing so light gets in, check hinges and props, and keep the lid from slamming in wind.
