## What it's for

A pole pruner (pole saw) puts a pruning saw, and often a hook-and-blade cutter, on a long shaft so you can cut high branches from the ground.

## How to use it

Stand out of the drop line, set your feet, and make controlled cuts. For heavier limbs, undercut first. Do not use a pole pruner near overhead power lines, and do not over-reach from a ladder.

## Care and maintenance

Clean the cutter and saw, dry them, and check the shaft locks and ropes or rods before storage (Oregon State University Extension; University of Wisconsin–Madison Extension).
