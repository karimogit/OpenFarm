## What it's for

A watering can is a portable container with a handle and a spout for watering plants by hand, from about 0.5 litres for houseplants to 10 litres for the garden. A rose on the spout breaks the stream into droplets so it doesn't wash out soil or damage delicate seedlings.

## How to use it

Water the soil at the base of plants rather than the leaves. Use the rose, turned upwards, for seed beds and seedlings; take it off to deliver water to a single plant.

## Care and maintenance

Empty it after use and store it out of frost so it doesn't split; clean a blocked rose.
