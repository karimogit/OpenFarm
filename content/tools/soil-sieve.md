## What it's for

A soil sieve (riddle) is a mesh screen for removing stones and clods from potting mix or seed compost, and for covering fine seed with a light, even layer (University of Minnesota Extension, starting seeds indoors).

## How to use it

Shake or rub the mix through the mesh into a tray or barrow. Use a finer mesh for seed covering than for general potting soil. Do not force wet, sticky soil through the screen.

## Care and maintenance

Tap out debris, dry the mesh, and brush it so it does not rust or clog.
