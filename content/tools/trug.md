## What it's for

A trug is a shallow, usually oval garden basket, traditionally of wood, for carrying harvested vegetables, fruit, flowers or small tools without piling them deeply and bruising them.

## How to use it

Lay produce in a single layer when you can, especially soft fruit and tomatoes. It is a carrier, not a digging tool.

## Care and maintenance

Empty it after use, let it dry, and keep a wooden trug out of standing water so the slats do not rot.
