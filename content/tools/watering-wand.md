## What it's for

A watering wand is a long, usually valved tube with a rose or breaker on the end, fitted to a hose, so you can water the soil at the base of plants without wetting foliage as much as a spray nozzle, and without stooping as much as a can.

## How to use it

Hold the rose close to the soil and water slowly so it soaks in. Use a fine rose on seedlings. Shut the valve when you move so you do not waste water or wash soil off roots.

## Care and maintenance

Drain it after use, especially before frost, and clear a blocked rose. Store it under cover.
