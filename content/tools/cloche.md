## What it's for

A cloche is a portable cover — glass, plastic or fabric on a frame — that shelters seedlings and low crops from wind and light frost and warms the soil in spring.

## How to use it

Set it over the row or plant before cold nights, and ventilate on sunny days so heat does not build up. Anchor it against wind. Remove or open it once the weather is reliably mild, so plants can harden off.

## Care and maintenance

Wash light-reducing dirt off the cover, and store frames so they do not crack. Check for pests that shelter underneath.
