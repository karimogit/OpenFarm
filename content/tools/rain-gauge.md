## What it's for

A rain gauge measures rainfall in the garden so you can judge whether to water. A week with useful rain may meet a crop's need; a dry spell will not, even if the air feels cool.

## How to use it

Stand it level in the open, away from buildings and overhanging trees, read it at a regular time, then empty it. Use the reading with how the soil feels at root depth, not as the only guide.

## Care and maintenance

Empty it after reading, rinse out silt, and bring plastic gauges in if hard frost might crack them.
