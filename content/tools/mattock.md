## What it's for

A mattock is a heavy digging tool with an adze-like blade, and often a pick or axe opposite, for breaking hard, rooty or compacted ground that a spade or fork cannot easily enter.

## How to use it

Swing the adze into the soil to chop roots and loosen clods, then lever or lift. Keep bystanders clear and use a firm stance. It is a land-clearing and hole-starting tool, not a fine seedbed rake.

## Care and maintenance

Clean soil off the head, dry it, and keep the edge serviceable. Check the head is tight on the handle before each use (Iowa State University Extension; University of Wisconsin–Madison Extension).
