## What it's for

A stirrup hoe (oscillating or scuffle hoe) has a stirrup-shaped blade that cuts just under the soil on both the push and the pull stroke, suited to shallow weeding in rows.

## How to use it

Keep the blade nearly flat and skim below the surface while weeds are small, walking backwards so you do not tread the loosened soil. It is not meant for chopping deep, established roots.

## Care and maintenance

Clean and dry the blade, sharpen the edges, and oil the pivot if it has one (Iowa State University Extension; University of Wisconsin–Madison Extension).
