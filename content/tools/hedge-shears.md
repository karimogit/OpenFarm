## What it's for

Hand hedge shears have long, straight blades for trimming hedges and shaping leafy growth, not for cutting thick branches (use loppers or a saw for those).

## How to use it

Keep the blades nearly parallel to the hedge face and cut little and often so you are only taking young growth. Avoid cutting into old bare wood on species that do not resprout readily.

## Care and maintenance

Wipe and dry the blades, sharpen the cutting edges, and oil the pivot (Oregon State University Extension; University of Wisconsin–Madison Extension).
