## What it's for

Stakes (cane, wood or metal) hold upright plants that would otherwise flop or break — tomatoes, some peppers, tall flowers and young trees — and can carry string for a simple trellis.

## How to use it

Drive the stake soon after planting, a little way from the stem, and tie the plant with a soft tie in a figure-eight so the stem is not chafed. Add ties as it grows. For heavy fruit, use a stake stout enough for a wet plant in wind.

## Care and maintenance

Clean soil and ties off at season's end, and discard rotten or split timber.
