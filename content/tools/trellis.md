## What it's for

A trellis is a framework of wood, metal or netting that supports climbing and sprawling plants — peas, beans, cucumbers, some tomatoes — keeping fruit off the soil and making picking and air flow easier.

## How to use it

Put the support in at planting time so you do not damage roots later. Train shoots onto the mesh or twine as they grow, and choose a structure rated for the crop's weight when wet.

## Care and maintenance

Brush off plant debris at the end of the season, and check posts and ties for rot or rust before the next crop.
