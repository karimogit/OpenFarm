## What it's for

A wheelbarrow carries soil, compost, mulch, tools and harvests around the garden. Single-wheel models manoeuvre in narrow paths; two-wheel models are more stable with heavy loads.

## How to use it

Load weight over the wheel, not the handles, and do not overload. On slopes, control the load from the uphill side where you can. Park it so it cannot roll.

## Care and maintenance

Tip out debris, dry the tray, keep the tyre inflated or the wheel bearings clean, and store it under cover (Iowa State University Extension).
