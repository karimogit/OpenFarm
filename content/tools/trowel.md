## What it's for

A garden trowel is a small hand tool for digging, moving or loosening small amounts of soil – planting seedlings, bulbs and small plants and potting up.

## How to use it

Dig the hole with a scooping motion, set the plant in and firm the soil with your hands. A narrow transplanting trowel is easier in crowded beds and pots.

## Care and maintenance

Clean off soil, dry it and oil the blade; sand rough wooden handles and wipe with linseed oil. Store it dry.
