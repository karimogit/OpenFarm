## What it's for

A hoe shapes soil, removes weeds and clears the soil of old roots and crop residues. It is also used for hilling soil around plants (potatoes, leeks) and drawing seed drills and shallow trenches.

## How to use it

For weeding, skim the blade just below the surface to cut weeds from their roots, ideally on a dry day while the weeds are small. Use the corner to draw a drill for sowing.

## Care and maintenance

A sharp hoe works much better: file the edge with a mill file at the original angle (about 30° if a new edge is needed) and remove the burr on the back. Clean and oil the blade, and store it dry.
