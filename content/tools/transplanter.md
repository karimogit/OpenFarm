## What it's for

A transplanter is a narrow trowel or scoop, sometimes marked for depth, for lifting seedlings and making a hole to set them in the bed (University of Minnesota Extension; Iowa State University Extension).

## How to use it

Water the cells first, lift the plant with as much root as you can, set it at the same depth (or slightly deeper for tomatoes), and firm the soil. Transplant on a dull, calm day or evening when you can.

## Care and maintenance

Clean and dry the blade after use, and oil metal parts before storage.
