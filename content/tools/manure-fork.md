## What it's for

A manure or compost fork (a pitchfork with several curved tines) lifts and turns loose material — compost, manure, mulch and hay — rather than digging heavy soil (that is a garden fork's job).

## How to use it

Slide the tines under the pile and lift; do not lever against buried stones as you would with a digging fork. It is also handy for shaking compost to aerate it.

## Care and maintenance

Clean organic matter off the tines, dry the tool, and store it under cover (Iowa State University Extension).
