## What it's for

A row cover (garden fleece or insect mesh) is a light fabric laid over crops to trap a little warmth, break the wind, or exclude some insect pests, depending on the material.

## How to use it

Drape it loosely so plants can lift it as they grow, or hoop it above the crop, and bury or peg the edges. Lift it for weeding and, for insect netting, before insect-pollinated crops need pollinators. Remove heavy covers once frost risk has passed if plants need more light.

## Care and maintenance

Dry the fabric before folding, mend tears, and store it out of strong sun, which weakens many textiles.
