## What it's for

A soaker hose weeps water slowly along its length so moisture soaks into the soil around plant roots, with less wetting of leaves than overhead sprinkling.

## How to use it

Lay it on the soil along a row or around a bed, preferably under mulch, and run it long enough for water to reach the root zone. Check for even wetting; kinks and uphill stretches water poorly. Follow any pressure-limit printed on the hose.

## Care and maintenance

Drain it before frost, and store it out of strong sun when you can, which lengthens the life of porous hose.
