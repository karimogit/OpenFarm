## What it's for

Pruning shears – hand pruners or secateurs – are strong scissors for plants. They cut branches up to about 2 cm thick; loppers are the larger two-handed version for thicker branches.

## How to use it

Cut just above a bud or side branch with the blade side towards the part you keep. Don't twist to force a cut – use loppers or a saw for thicker wood.

## Care and maintenance

Remove sap with soapy water or alcohol, and disinfect blades (70% alcohol) between diseased plants. Sharpen with a fine file or stone at the original bevel (about 20–25°), with the tool clamped. Take apart, clean, sharpen and adjust at the start, middle and end of the pruning season; oil the pivot and blades.
