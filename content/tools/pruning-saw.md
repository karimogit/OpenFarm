## What it's for

A pruning saw cuts branches that are too thick for secateurs or loppers. Folding and fixed-blade saws with coarse teeth designed to cut on the pull stroke are the usual garden types.

## How to use it

Support the branch, start with an undercut on heavier limbs so the bark does not tear, then cut down from the top just outside the branch collar. Let the teeth do the work; do not bend the blade in the cut.

## Care and maintenance

Brush out sawdust, dry the blade, and keep the teeth clean. Store a folding saw closed and dry (Oregon State University Extension; University of Wisconsin–Madison Extension).
