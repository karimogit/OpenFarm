## What it's for

A hori-hori, or soil knife, is a heavy steel blade, sharp and often serrated on the sides, with a semi-sharp point. It digs, cuts roots, weeds, divides plants and opens bags.

## How to use it

Use the point to dig small planting holes or lever out tap-rooted weeds; use the serrated edge to cut roots or divide clumps. Keep it in a sheath: both edges are sharp.

## Care and maintenance

Clean and dry it after use; remove sap with soapy water or alcohol. Sharpen the plain edge with a file or whetstone with the tool clamped in a vise, and oil the blade.
