## What it's for

A garden or bow rake has short, stiff tines on a wide head for levelling soil, breaking clods and spreading mulch or gravel. A leaf rake has longer, flexible tines for gathering leaves and light debris without digging into the soil.

## How to use it

For a seedbed, pull a bow rake over loosened soil to level it and remove stones. Flip it to use the back of the head as a smoother. Use a leaf rake with a light pulling motion so you do not tear into the soil or turf.

## Care and maintenance

Knock off soil and leaves, dry the head, and store it off the ground. Straighten bent tines and oil metal parts before winter (Iowa State University Extension; University of Wisconsin–Madison Extension).
