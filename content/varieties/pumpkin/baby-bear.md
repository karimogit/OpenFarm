Baby bear pumpkins have deep orange fruits, weighting 600g to 1 kg fruits with slender, sturdy, easy to grip handles. In addition to its decorative use, the flesh is good for pies, and the semi hull-less seeds are suitable for toasted snacks. The yield potential is high, and the fruits have outstanding storage qualities.
