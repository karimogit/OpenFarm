The Royal Horticultural Society groups cabbages by harvesting season. Winter cabbages provide harvests when little else is available. RHS says they include crinkly Savoy types and smooth drumheads, as well as red-tinged or purple varieties, and that they usually form large heads and need plenty of space. Sow in late spring or early summer. Once mature, they often stand in good condition for months, or can be stored in a cool, frost-free place. “Savoy” and “drumhead” are recorded here as RHS wording within this seasonal class, not as separate groups we have named ourselves.

This page uses the RHS harvesting-season class, not a botanical rank. Individual cultivars are only listed here when a source places them in this class.
