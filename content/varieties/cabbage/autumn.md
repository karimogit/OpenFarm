The Royal Horticultural Society groups cabbages by harvesting season. Autumn cabbages often form large heads and need wider spacing. RHS says to sow them in mid-spring to early summer (April–June outdoors, together with winter types) and to harvest before winter. Some varieties can be stored for use in winter.

This page uses the RHS harvesting-season class, not a botanical rank. Individual cultivars are only listed here when a source places them in this class.
