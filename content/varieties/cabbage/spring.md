The Royal Horticultural Society groups cabbages by harvesting season. Spring cabbages usually form small, dense, pointed heads. RHS says to sow them in July for overwintering, and either to pick them as loose spring greens or to leave them to form heads from mid-spring to mid-summer. Young plants may be set closer at first and then thinned in late February or March, with the thinnings used as spring greens.

This page uses the RHS harvesting-season class, not a botanical rank. Individual cultivars are only listed here when a source places them in this class.
