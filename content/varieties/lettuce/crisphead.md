The Royal Horticultural Society treats crisphead as one of three main hearting lettuce types. Crispheads produce large hearts of curled, crisp leaves with a mild flavour. RHS says this type is more resistant to bolting and stores well in the fridge, and that it includes the popular iceberg lettuces.

“Iceberg” is recorded here as RHS’s wording for lettuces within this type, not as a separate group we have named ourselves. This page uses the RHS horticultural type, not a botanical rank. Individual cultivars are only listed here when a source places them in this type.
