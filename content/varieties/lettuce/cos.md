The Royal Horticultural Society treats cos lettuce (also known as romaine) as one of three main hearting types. Cos forms an upright, oblong head, and the leaves have a crisp mid-rib. RHS notes that the type comes in various sizes, including compact ‘Little Gem’.

Hearting lettuces generally take up to three months to reach harvest size. RHS also lists cos, with crisphead, among types that are often more resistant to premature flowering (bolting). This page uses the RHS horticultural type, not a botanical rank. Individual cultivars are only listed here when a source places them in this type.
