The Royal Horticultural Society distinguishes loose-leaf lettuces and salad-leaf mixes from hearting types. Loose-leaf plants produce a less dense rosette of foliage, suited to picking individual leaves and to small spaces and containers. They are quicker than hearting types: RHS says the first leaves can often be picked about a month after sowing, and picking can continue for several weeks as a cut-and-come-again crop.

This page uses the RHS horticultural type, not a botanical rank. Salad-leaf mixes are mentioned on the same RHS page but are not treated here as a separate named group. Individual cultivars are only listed here when a source places them in this type.
