The Royal Horticultural Society treats butterhead as one of three main hearting lettuce types. Butterheads have an open shape and soft, juicy leaves with a mild flavour. RHS says they are fairly quick to mature, tolerate poorer growing conditions, and are best eaten fresh because they do not store well.

Hearting lettuces, including butterheads, generally take up to three months to reach harvest size, when the whole head is cut. This page uses the RHS horticultural type, not a botanical rank. Individual cultivars are only listed here when a source places them in this type.
