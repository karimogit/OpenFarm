Yellow onions have with yellow skin and white to yellow flesh. They are members of the Allium family along with garlic and leeks. They have a good balance of sweet and astringency in their flavor, and become sweeter when cooked. Spanish onions are a type of yellow onion that is slightly sweeter and more delicate in flavor. Make sure to choose a variety suited to your day length.
