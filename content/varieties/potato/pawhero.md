(early, short season cultivar) This potato is long and curved like a sausage, with very bright purple skin like Maori, and with white flesh like Maori, which is also fluffy like Maori when cooked. It has been widespread around NZ in the past and has come to us from many people. [Info from Koanga Institute, used with permission]
