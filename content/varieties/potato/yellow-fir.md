(early short season cultivar) Similar to Pink Fir, but with yellow skin, outstanding long smooth-skinned buttery potatoes, make excellent potato salad, cam here with our Irish ancestors! This line is the same as La Ratte, a traditional French potato that has been in New Zealand for a long time. It is very very similar to Yellow Fir, a yellow waxy finger type, that produces all it's potatoes right beside the stem in one pile. They are buttery delicious. An early cropper. also called Lady Finger.
