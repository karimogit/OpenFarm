The Royal Horticultural Society divides early (‘new’) potatoes into first or second earlies. Second earlies are planted a little later than first earlies — in early to mid-April in the UK — and are generally ready in July and August.

As with other earlies, the plants are smaller than maincrop types and can be grown in containers. Chitting is described as worthwhile for early varieties. This page uses the RHS harvest-timing class, not a botanical rank. Individual cultivars are only listed here when a source places them in this group.
