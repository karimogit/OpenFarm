The Royal Horticultural Society classes early potatoes as ‘new potatoes’ and further divides them into first or second earlies. First earlies are the faster early group: in the UK they are typically planted around late March and lifted in June and July, often when the tubers are about the size of hens’ eggs.

RHS notes that early plants take up less space than maincrops, can be grown in containers, and are usually harvested by midsummer, which frees the ground for a later crop. Chitting (pre-sprouting) seed potatoes is described as worthwhile for early varieties, though not essential.

This page uses the RHS harvest-timing class, not a botanical rank. Individual cultivars are only listed here when a source places them in this group.
