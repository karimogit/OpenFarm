The Royal Horticultural Society contrasts early potatoes with maincrop varieties, which stay in the ground longer, through late summer or early autumn. In the UK they are typically planted from mid-April to early May. Harvest can start in late August for immediate use; if the plants stay healthy, tubers may be left until mid-autumn and stored.

RHS describes maincrops as producing a larger harvest and bigger tubers, useful for roasting and baking. Chitting is not considered an advantage for this longer-season group. This page uses the RHS harvest-timing class, not a botanical rank. Existing imported cultivar records are not placed in this group unless a source says they belong here.
