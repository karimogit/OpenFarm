Blocky elongated cream skin with a lot of purples covering, turning almost to a golden russet, when fully mature. A new cultivar bred by Koanga Institute from the aerial seed. This is an elongated potato oval shaped with a purple smooth skin and white eyes. It was named by a local kaumatua.
