Pineapple tomatoes are an heirloom variety that produce large fruit that can weigh 1-2lbs and measure at least 12cm in diameter. Tomatoes are bi-colored with a streaked red and yellow exterior. Flesh is swirled with red, pink, orange, and yellow and meaty with few seeds. They have a sweet fruity taste with a hint of citrus. For larger yield and improved plant health, prune and stake plants.
