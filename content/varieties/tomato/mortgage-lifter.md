Developed in the 1930's by "Radiator Charlie" Byles of West Virginia. Charlie sold plants for $1.00 and paid off his $6000 mortgage in 6 years. Extremely large, slightly flat, pink fruit can grow to 1 kilo. This variety is famous for its productivity and disease resistance. Indeterminate. Matures 85 days from transplant.
