Striped Cavern is an heirloom tomato variety that produces medium-sized, thick-walled, hollow tomatoes similar to bell peppers. The tomatoes are often stuffed or added to fresh salsas. They have red skin with yellow and orange stripes and a sweet, mild taste. For larger yield and improved plant health, prune and stake plants.
