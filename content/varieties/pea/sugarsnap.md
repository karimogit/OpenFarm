The Royal Horticultural Society treats sugarsnap peas as one of the main pea types, grown in a similar way to garden (shelling) peas and mangetouts. Sugarsnaps have fleshy, rounded pods and are eaten pod and all, picked before the peas mature. RHS says mangetout and sugarsnap types are the easiest to grow and tend to produce larger crops, and that they should be picked when the pods are about 7.5 cm long, just as the peas start to develop.

This page uses the RHS horticultural type, not a botanical rank. Individual cultivars are only listed here when a source places them in this type.
