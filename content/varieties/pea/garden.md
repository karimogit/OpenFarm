The Royal Horticultural Society distinguishes peas for shelling, known as garden peas, from mangetouts and sugarsnaps. Garden peas are grown for the seeds inside the pod. RHS says they produce either smooth or wrinkled peas: smooth varieties are hardier and better for early sowings; wrinkled varieties are for late spring or summer sowing and have a sweeter flavour.

Within garden peas, RHS also classes varieties as first earlies, second earlies or maincrops by how long they take to start cropping (about 12 weeks, 13–14 weeks, or 14–16 weeks). Those timing classes are described in the temperate oceanic pea guide and are not recorded here as separate groups. This page uses the RHS horticultural type, not a botanical rank.
